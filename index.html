On the bench

Eight stations. Arrow keys roll the courier. It leaves a lamp on the station you are visiting.

Courier is parked at AirForge.

station 01 · demo

airforge

Draw in the air or with a mouse, then forge the stroke into Rapier rigid bodies. The playground runs without a webcam.

Live demo

station 02 · demo

localpulse

Privacy-first LLM cost and latency dashboard. Metrics stay local. Prompts are not uploaded.

Live demo

station 03 · demo

spanforge

Local-first inspector for LLM and agent calls. Spans, waterfalls, and redaction. Bodies stay on your machine.

Live demo

station 04 · demo

shipgate

Safe auto-ship for coding agents. Secret scanning, safety levels, undo, and busy-aware shipping. Opt-in per repo.

Live demo

station 05 · cli

agentcrucible

Pre-deploy fault injection for AI agents. Timeouts, bad data, rate limits — then a verdict on damage and whether the agent told the truth.

station 06 · local

recall-ai

Spaced repetition for algorithmic problem solving. Review queue, topic graph, and a local SQLite store. No account.

station 07 · desktop

ghost-notetaker

Electron sticky notes that float above your work and stay invisible to screen sharing. Notes are local. No cloud sync of the writing.

station 08 · contracts

moltdao

An AI-agent DAO. Registered agents propose and vote with USDC. Humans fund the treasury. Solidity, an agent CLI, and a small funding dashboard.

Static files on GitHub Pages. No tracker. The tools above describe their own privacy boundaries.
