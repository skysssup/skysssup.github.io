- **Content is HTML first.** Every word a reviewer can read must be in the committed HTML, not injected by JavaScript. Crawlers, share previews, and no-JS visitors see the same text.

- **One accent per mode. Nothing set above 24px.** Instrument Sans and Fragment Mono only. The grid is a fixed sheet and content aligns to it.
- **Original everything.** Do not copy code, text, images, or UI from luffy.sh, sumitx.me, or any other portfolio. Study them for feel only.
- **Write from the code.** Every number, module name, and mechanism in a case study must be verifiable in the repository. If something is unknown, leave it out or ask Aakash. Never invent users, metrics, employers, credentials, dates, or roadmap items.

Anything deeper than that goes to Aakash as a numbered fact-by-fact list for approval before publishing. Never publish secrets, tokens, env values, hostnames, emails from git metadata, wallet or contract addresses, or machine paths.
- **No dates anywhere** unless Aakash supplies real timelines (git history starts 2026-09-29 for all eight repos, which is not the real story).
- **Keep every test green** (`npm test`, `npm run test:e2e`) and add tests for every behavior you add. CI runs both on every push.
- **Verify visually after every major change:** 1440, 1280, 768, 390 × light, dark, Gear Two, for every page, with console errors, overflow, and axe at zero. Critique screenshots like a design director and fix before moving on.
- Commit in coherent steps with real messages. Push to `main` when a step is green and verified live.

## 5. Hero and motion upgrades (measure before and after)

The hero is good; make it exceptional without making it heavier.

> **Status after the second hero pass ([next-2.md](next-2.md)), all on `main`:** the cat is gone; a band of light
> and a burst of four-point stars cross the figure at each turn of the sway; Gear Two keeps tearing the figure
> on about a third of its heartbeats, with a long tear about every 6 s; the source is the 424 px avatar
> upscaled 4×, with Depth Anything V2 Base depth and an 896 px ink map, so the face and hands read
> (22,730 → 93,015 dots at 1440 × 900, 6,292 → 25,707 at 390, JS still 0.3–0.4 ms a frame); the ring carries
> one line in the statue's voice and brightens on a theme hover; and the figure is colored from a material map
> and a palette designed per mode. The design spec (§5, §7–§9, §14) describes the result.

- **Dot density and depth.** Done in the second pass: `DENSITY = 0.85` on an 896 px ink map weighted by local contrast, `resolutionFor` 1.4×scale (320–1200 cells), and a detail value that shrinks dots on fine features. Try a two-layer approach: a dense fine layer for form plus a sparse larger layer for highlights; or anisotropic point sizes along the depth gradient so the engraving reads as hatching. Keep ≤ 2 draw calls and judge on 2× screenshots at 1440 and 390 in all three modes.
- **Lighting.** Done: a normal per dot is derived from the depth map at load (`depthNormals` in `js/hero.js`) and interleaved into the one vertex buffer; the vertex shader rotates it with the figure and applies a Lambert term from the upper left plus a rim term, so the shading moves as the figure turns.
- **Load.** The assemble-from-shell is 1.4 s; try center-out with a slight overshoot and make the ring fade in after the figure settles (currently 1.2–1.9 s). Avoid any flash of empty space: show `still.webp` under the canvas until the first WebGL frame (`is-live` already toggles it; verify there's no gap at 390).
- **Ring.** Letters at the back currently draw mirrored and dim. Consider culling back glyphs entirely when they overlap the figure (done) and rendering the rest with a 3D-correct skew; also test one lap of smaller text (10px at 390) for legibility. Keep names whole, always.
- **Cursor and touch.** Push radius is `max(70, size*0.13)` px. Add gentle inertia so the field keeps moving for ~300 ms after the cursor stops. On touch: a tap ripples (done); add a slow drag to rotate (yaw/pitch follow finger, spring back) without hijacking vertical scroll (`touch-action: pan-y` must stay).
- **Gear Two.** Done, and more: the afterimage (two extra draws at ±2 px, 30% alpha, during the glitch), tiles torn out of the figure with red frames, a ripple from the centre as the palette turns, hot dots on each beat, the ring breathing 2% with the heartbeat, a red glow and a pulsing switch phased to the same clock (`--beat-delay`), a shockwave ring from the switch, text split and media slices page-wide. - **Reduced motion.** Still frame + no ring spin is right. Make sure switching reduced motion on mid-animation redraws exactly once.
- **Fallbacks.** Add a WebGL context-lost handler (`webglcontextlost` → show still, `webglcontextrestored` → rebuild). Add a visibility test: tab hidden stops RAF (already) and resumes without a time jump (clamp `dt`, already 50 ms; verify ring `spin` doesn't jump).
